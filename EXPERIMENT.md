# Experiment brief

## Hypothesis

For **[specific audience]**, **[single interaction]** will cause **[observable behaviour]** because **[reason]**.

## One primary action

Describe the one thing a visitor should do. Anything not required for that action is out of scope for the three-day build.

## Validation window

- Build starts: YYYY-MM-DD
- Launches: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision due: YYYY-MM-DD (14 days after launch)
- Minimum distribution: 7 original X posts, 35 relevant replies, 20 individually researched sponsor approaches

## Decision gates

- Scale: at least 300 unique visitors and either 8% sharing, 10% returning players, or one paid sponsor.
- Iterate once: at least 100 visitors plus one strong engagement signal or three qualified sponsor replies.
- Kill: fewer than 100 visitors after minimum distribution, no payment, and no qualified sponsor interest.
